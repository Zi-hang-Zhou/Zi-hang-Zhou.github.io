I am an undergraduate in the School of Artificial Intelligence at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and an intern at Microsoft Research Asia.

My work spans automated repository setup, future-event forecasting, workspace-agent evaluation, and multimodal data analysis. I am particularly interested in how agents reuse experience—and how we verify that they actually accomplish their tasks.
